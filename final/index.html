Read together

Find your next book and someone to talk about it with.

Libro Cercano is a small reading club idea for people who love books, want simple recommendations, and need help finding where a book may be available in Ecuador.

Browse Books

Why this club

Sometimes I finish a book and want to share what I felt, but I do not always have someone nearby who has read it. This site helps readers start that conversation.

What it does

The book list shows titles, authors, genres, and a possible local place to start looking. It is a simple guide for readers who want ideas.

Reader goal

The goal is to build a calm space where reading feels social, useful, and easier to keep as a habit.

Books and places

The directory connects books with possible bookstores or reading places. It is not a live store inventory, but it gives readers a starting point.
